🪙 $VMH — the token

Venomeum (VMH) is live on testnets. ERC-20, 1,000,000,000 supply, 18 decimals. Tax applies only to DEX trades (never wallet-to-wallet): 1% burned, 1% automatic liquidity, 1% automatic buyback — the buyback slice auto-swaps to the chain's native token, buys VMH back from the pool, and burns it.

🦄 Venomeum Swap — our own pools

Neither testnet had a DEX, so we deployed our own Uniswap V2 (factory 0x6e0F7bB0AfA4f4e0208e9A6F06a8759003E2D184, router 0xC83d7A0079Fc410Ddd4cE4aaD7bF2216350342Cb — same addresses on both chains) and launched the pools on it: 1,000,000 VMH against 1 USDC on Arc and 0.001 ETH on Robinhood. Liquidity is removable — LP tokens stay in the provider's wallet, withdrawable at any time; we proved it by withdrawing 10% of every pool again after launch. Real taxed trades already ran through the pools: roughly 2,500 VMH burned per deployment from the burn tax plus the automatic buyback, and the auto-LP topped the pools up on its own.
